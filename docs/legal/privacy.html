Privacy Policy

Korb for Android (package com.shoppingapp) · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Korb is a shopping-list app that works on your device and needs no account. It does not show advertising and does not include analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

Optional accounts and sync

You can use the app entirely without an account. Two optional account types exist:

  • Sign in with Google — the app receives your Google account's email address and display name and uses Google Drive's app-private folder as the backup location. The app can only see its own folder inside your Drive and cannot read your other files. There is no developer-operated server: the data goes directly from your device to your own Google account.
  • Local email account — the email address and a PBKDF2-SHA256 hash of your password are stored in the app's private storage on that device. The password itself is never stored and the account never leaves the phone.

Signing out removes the account reference from the device. Data already stored in your Google Drive folder stays there until you delete it in Drive.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your shopping list, pantry stock, purchase history, budget and Pfand entries, saved offers, cached flyer pages and local backups in its private app storage. This data is not uploaded to a server operated by the developer; it only leaves the device if you sign in with Google and start a sync.

PDF reports are created on the device and kept in the app cache until you share them or clear app data. If you activate an optional premium licence, only the signed licence token is stored locally; it contains no payment details.

Network requests

The app makes network requests only for app functionality:

  • Barcode lookup: the barcode you scan is sent to world.openfoodfacts.org to retrieve a product name.
  • Flyers: opening the offers screen requests marktguru.de and its image CDN to download publicly available flyer pages.

Like any internet request, these requests necessarily include your IP address and a user-agent string. No account identifier, contact detail or shopping-list content is included. The providers process requests under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Camera (optional): used only to scan barcodes and receipts. Photos are processed on the device and are not uploaded by the app.
  • Notifications (optional): used only for reminders you enable.
  • Internet: required for barcode lookup and flyers.

Backups

You can export your data as a JSON file to a location you choose (for example Google Drive or Downloads), and the app keeps up to five automatic local backups in its private storage. Backup files are not encrypted; anyone with access to the file can read it. Share backups only with people you trust.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. In the app, open Backup and settings and choose Delete all data to permanently remove the database, local backups, cached flyer images and the crash log. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. Exported backup files remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately.

Diagnostics

If the app crashes, a technical log (app version, Android version, thread name and stack trace) is written to private app storage. It contains no shopping data and is only shared if you explicitly tap Share diagnostic log.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
